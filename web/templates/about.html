What it does

Why the workspace exists
Cash Machine Engine is built to reduce the time between "I found an idea" and "I know whether I should build it". It combines crawling, analysis, scoring, trend detection and portfolio organization into one founder workspace.
The goal is simple: help founders identify small but realistic software opportunities that can reach meaningful monthly revenue without wasting weeks on dead ends.
1 Find opportunities from multiple public sources.
2 Score them using structured AI analysis.
3 Prioritize what deserves attention now.
4 Track momentum, portfolio buckets and reports.
